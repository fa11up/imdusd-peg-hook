Job `442fd649-f535-4fd4-b3d7-bcffafa490b9` (https://explorer.imd.fun/jobs/442fd649-f535-4fd4-b3d7-bcffafa490b9),

**No finding in the claims, `sweep` or `unlockCallback`.** Every finding above info concerned the empty-pool guard.

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | medium | The guard ran only while active liquidity was exactly zero; one wei of full-range liquidity switched it off for good, so the first real deposit could still land at a moved price. All five seats. | **Guard removed** with its flag. The deposit carries its own bounds instead: PositionManager's `amount0Max` / `amount1Max` from `depositMaxes()`, computed at $1; at a moved price one side exceeds its maximum and the mint reverts, dust liquidity or not (`test_aBoundedDepositRefusesAMovedPool`). |
| 2 | low | The guard blocked every add whenever the price sat outside all positions and outside the band, where restoring is not free. | **Gone** with the guard. |
| 3 | low | While the pool was empty, a free swap could front-run every add, so the first liquidity could be blocked indefinitely. | **Gone** with the guard; a restore and a bounded deposit can go in one transaction (Universal Router: a V4 swap, then the PositionManager call). |
| 4 | low | The end-price rate is sandwichable: a trade placed just before a swap pushes it past the band edge, so it pays the ramp. | **Accepted and documented:** the surcharge goes to the Treasury, not to the sandwicher, and the swap's own price limit bounds it. |
| 5 | info | `checkPrice()` refused adds the hook would accept. | **Removed;** replaced by `depositMaxes()`. |
| 6 | info | The README listed three flag bits while the address needed four. | **Moot:** with the guard removed the address carries three again; the README names them. |
| 7 | info | The constructor needs code at both token addresses, so the protected launch floor cannot build it on a bare chain. | **Accepted:** the hook is deployed by our own script on mainnet, not through a launch. |
